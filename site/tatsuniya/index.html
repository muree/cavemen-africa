Tatsuniya Creative Workshop

Every child has a story to tell. Tatsuniya brings the tradition of storytelling into a creative space where children can listen, imagine, create and learn how to share their own stories using simple modern tools.

About Tatsuniya

Tatsuniya is the Hausa word for storytelling. Storytelling has historically been a way for communities to pass down wisdom, values, memories and stories from one generation to another.

This workshop brings that tradition into a space where children can move from simply listening to stories to creating their own. Through storytelling, creative writing and simple phone video recording, children are encouraged to imagine, express themselves and share their ideas.

The workshop connects the old with the new: traditional stories and values provide the foundation, while modern creative tools give children new ways to tell their stories.

What children will explore

  • Traditional stories, values and oral storytelling.
  • How to develop simple story ideas and characters.
  • Basic creative and script writing.
  • Public speaking and telling stories with confidence.
  • How to use a phone to record a short storytelling video.
  • Working with other children to create and share ideas.

Workshop details

Tatsuniya is designed as a five-day creative workshop for children, combining storytelling, creative expression and simple digital tools.

Duration

5 days

Focus

Storytelling, creative writing, expression and phone video.

Approach

Children learn by imagining, creating, practising and sharing.

Frequently asked questions

What is Tatsuniya?

Tatsuniya is the Hausa word for storytelling. The workshop uses storytelling as a starting point for helping children develop their imagination, confidence and creative expression.

What will the children learn?

Children will explore storytelling, simple story and script writing, public speaking and basic phone video recording. They will also have opportunities to create and share their own stories.

Do children need filmmaking experience?

No. The workshop is designed around simple, practical activities. Children will be introduced to phone video recording as part of the storytelling process.

How long is the workshop?

The Tatsuniya Creative Workshop runs for five days. More information about the programme, dates and registration will be provided during registration.
