About us

Cavemen Africa is a creative hub based in Northern Nigeria that brings people together through storytelling, culture, learning, and collaboration.

We create platforms, programs, and experiences that help creatives, entrepreneurs, and communities connect, grow, and share ideas. From workshops and exhibitions to discussions, screenings, mentorship programs, and original media productions, our work is rooted in the belief that creativity is not just art—it is a tool for learning, opportunity, and positive change.
