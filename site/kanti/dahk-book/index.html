Diary of a Heartbreak Kid

Timeline: 2011 to 2026

Diary of a Heartbreak Kid (DAHK) is a poetry anthology written over fifteen years, from 2011 to 2026. It moves through seven emotional seasons: Bloom, Wilt, Dry, Thorns, Ashes, Roots, and Epiphany.


The book is written by Nigerian-Ghanaian author Salamatu Adamu and comes from her private notebooks written over time, never originally meant to be published. Those notes eventually became a full collection. It follows a simple idea — how the heart changes through different stages of love, loss, silence, and recovery. Each season reflects a different point in that process.


What started as personal writing has grown into a complete body of work that brings together memory, experience, and reflection.

About the Author

Salamatu Adamu

Math Graduate of ABU Zaria • Storyteller & Photographer

Salamatu Adamu is a Nigerian-Ghanaian writer, photographer, and multi-faceted creative. A Mathematics graduate of Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, she bridges the gap between logic and lyricism, viewing storytelling as a vital tool for cultural preservation, economic resilience, and human connection.

Following the success of her first book, Epiphany Magic Book (Africa’s first handbook for SMEs on product presentation), Salamatu now unveils her most intimate and raw multimedia project yet to the public.

KANTI BY CAVEMEN AFRICA

Kanti is the Hausa word for a shop or market where people gather to exchange or buy valuables. At Cavemen Africa, it is more than a store — it is the home for the things we create, preserve, and share. It houses the creations of the Cavemen Africa ecosystem, where ideas become physical and accessible to people. In its simplest form, it is a shop; in practice, it is a living archive of our work, stories, and creations.
