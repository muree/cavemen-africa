KANTI BY CAVEMEN AFRICA

Kanti is the Hausa word for a shop or market where people gather to exchange or buy valuables. At Cavemen Africa, it is more than a store — it is the home for the things we create, preserve, and share. It houses the creations of the Cavemen Africa ecosystem, where ideas become physical and accessible to people. In its simplest form, it is a shop; in practice, it is a living archive of our work, stories, and creations.
