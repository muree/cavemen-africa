The Weight of Dreams: Cavemen Africa Debuts the Nation’s First Structured Spoken Word Campus Tour

After a year of Asali Poetry Sessions — 9 editions and 282 poets at the Cave — the Asali Campus Tour opened at ABU Zaria on August 1st, 2026.

Cavemen Africa

August 5, 2026

After a year of organizing Asali Poetry Sessions, 9 editions and hosting 282 poets at the Cave, Cavemen Africa has officially launched the Asali Campus Tour. On August 1st, 2026, marking the first-ever structured spoken word campus tour in Nigeria, the tour made its debut at the iconic Drama Village, Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, and the result was a cultural shift that resonated across the entire campus.

A Year of Preparation, A Day of Execution

This milestone was no accident. It was the culmination of a year of impact, and to pull it off, we leaned into the strength of the community we’ve built. The ABU Zaria edition was fully self-sponsored by Cavemen Africa, a strategic decision to bet on our vision and the power of storytelling.

To bring this high-stakes production to life, we assembled a powerhouse team of over a dozen professional talents across production, project management, branding and design, media and communications, operations, and event management. This core crew was joined by over a dozen dedicated student volunteers from ABU, who served as the heartbeat of the on-ground logistics. After months of ideation, weeks of intense planning, and hours of simulation, we delivered a flawless three-hour program that set a new gold standard for campus events.

The Great Samaru Resonates

The response from the university was unprecedented. ABU Zaria proved to be an incredibly supportive and responsive host, with the Faculty of Arts providing institutional backing that gave the event immense weight.

The crowd of 142 attendees—comprising students, faculty, and creative enthusiasts—packed the Drama Village and remained locked in from start to finish. It was an electric atmosphere: every metaphor was met with silence or snaps, and every punchline resonated through the amphitheater. The audience wasn’t just watching; they were part of the story.

This energy was matched by the presence of our distinguished special guests, including:

  • Prof. Sule Muhammad (Dean, Faculty of Arts)
  • Prof. Shehu Ismail Chafe (HOD, Fine Art)
  • Dr. Emmanuel Tsadu Gana (HOD, Theatre and Performing Arts)
  • Representatives from the Office of the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (DVC)
  • Representatives from Bookish Alchemist

The Sifting: Meet the Judges and the Legends

The integrity of the competition was held by a distinguished three-judge panel who brought academic and creative authority to the “sifting” process:

  • Dr. Abdulmalik Abubakar (Department of Theatre and Performing Arts, ABU Zaria)
  • Salamatu Adamu (Poet, Author, and Founding Partner at Cavemen Africa)
  • Dr. Isah Ibrahim (Head of Department, English and Literary Studies, ABU Zaria)

These judges witnessed a grueling battle among ten finalists who represented the incredible intellectual diversity of ABU.

Asali Campus Legend

Jatau Ovye John (King Revelation)

Automotive Engineering · ₦250,000 grand prize · 3-round poetry slam

Jatau Ovye John (King Revelation) emerged as the first-ever Asali Campus Legend after 3 rounds of the poetry slam, taking home the ₦250,000 grand prize. His victory, along with the outstanding performances of the runners-up, has etched their names into the permanent record of Asali.
